# UmaPilot v0.1.16

- Choose Full roster, Classic pair closure, or Advanced pair closure as separate project methods.
- Advanced pair closure prepares all four members under your acceptance settings, reuses qualifying owned veterans, and allows guests during preparation. Once ready, it runs classic closure with the prepared owned parents.
- Assign and swap loop members to choose your final pair. Active or uncollected careers are protected from rearrangement.
- Classic pair closure reuses an approved final partner when available. An optional seed-parent build can repair a missing qualified seed without losing the loop checkpoint.
- Show preparation progress, current phase, step and cycle. Guest searches during preparation cover all four loop members.
- Redesigned Accounts with larger controls, clearer connection and recovery sections, and a responsive layout.

Validated with 99 backend tests, 54 frontend tests, browser regressions and a production build. No live career was started or collected during testing. This code-only update excludes accounts, credentials, settings and run history.
