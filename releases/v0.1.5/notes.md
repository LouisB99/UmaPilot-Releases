# UmaPilot v0.1.5

Continuous Pilot now controls automatic career collection as well as starting the next career.

- Connecting with Continuous Pilot off leaves the waiting career untouched.
- Turning Pilot off cancels scheduled collection and blocks stale queued jobs.
- Switching off during completion pauses before the next automatic request.
- The dashboard clearly shows that automatic collection is paused.

Verified with 54 backend regression tests and an offline browser test. Your saved projects and accounts are preserved.
