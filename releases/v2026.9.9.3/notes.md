## Pilot account recovery fix

- Save encrypted credentials now submits the form and confirms success.
- Save failures are shown beside the form and can be retried.
- Newly added or recreated Steam profiles can restore a saved Pilot target through Data Link.
- First verification preserves the saved credentials for subsequent connections.
- Missing or Windows-bound recovery credentials now get an actionable error.

Choose Check for updates from the UmaPilot tray menu, then quit and reopen UmaPilot after the download finishes. Saved projects, accounts and results are preserved.
