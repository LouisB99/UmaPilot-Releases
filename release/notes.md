UmaPilot v0.1.11

Keep saved uma.moe credentials intact during unrelated settings saves. Reject blank key replacements, serialize overlapping saves, and ignore old polling responses. Show whether a key is missing, readable, or present but locked to another Windows account.

Show specific Discord delivery errors without exposing credentials. Saved webhook URLs that Windows cannot unlock are clearly identified.

The launcher now opens a dedicated Brave, Chrome, or Edge dashboard window and closes it when you quit UmaPilot. Regular browser tabs remain independent.

This update contains no personal data. A credential encrypted on another machine must be entered once on the VM; an update cannot decrypt it there.
